15-Hour Masterclass Framework: Integrating Modules & Practical Application

Class Topics

Total Allotted Time: 15 Hours

3 Specialist Teachers

Core Communication (6 Hrs)

The Press Conference Simulator

Modules: Verbal, Non-verbal, Written, Listening, Impromptu, Group Discussion, Conversation.

Blending Strategy

Instead of teaching speaking and listening separately, students participate in a mock "Press Conference". They must draft a written statement, present it (verbal/non-verbal), handle impromptu questions, and engage in interactive listening to respond accurately to "reporters".

On-Field Assignment

Community Interview: Conduct a 10-minute live interview with a local business owner or senior teacher. Analyze their non-verbal cues, practice interactive listening, and submit a written summary of the interaction.

Interpersonal Dynamics (5 Hrs)

The Crisis Mediation Roleplay

Modules: Questioning, Feedback (SWOT), Emotional Intelligence, Networking, Motivating, Conflict.

Blending Strategy

A masterclass revolving around "Workplace Crisis". Students are paired up to resolve a staged conflict. They must use Emotional Intelligence to gauge the room, apply the Art of Questioning to find the root cause, and deliver Constructive Feedback using the SWOT framework to motivate their peer.

On-Field Assignment

Networking Audit: Attend a school inter-house event or local gathering. Initiate 3 meaningful conversations using open-ended questioning. Report back with a personal SWOT analysis of your own networking performance.

Public Relations (4 Hrs)

The Campaign Pitch

Modules: PR/Dawat, Stakeholders, Media Relations, Event Planning, Ethics & Evaluating.

Blending Strategy

Students form PR agencies. They are tasked with planning a single Dawat/Community Event. In this 4-hour bootcamp, they identify stakeholders, craft the narrative, design a digital media strategy, and present an ethical evaluation plan for their proposed event.

On-Field Assignment

Mini PR Campaign: Execute a real 3-day PR campaign for a school club or local charity. Capture 5 photos/videos, write 2 social media captions aligned with the core narrative, and evaluate the audience engagement.

Total Allotted Time: 15 Hours

2 Specialist Teachers

Internal Mindset (7.5 Hrs)

The Resilience Audit

Modules: Self Awareness, Accepting Change, Positivity, Accountable, Humility, Criticism, Stress.

Blending Strategy

A deeply reflective bootcamp. Rather than lecturing on "humility", the teacher creates a "Feedback Sandbox" where students review each other's past work. Students practice taking criticism, managing their stress response in real-time, maintaining positivity, and writing a self-awareness journal detailing their emotional triggers.

On-Field Assignment

The Feedback Loop: Approach 3 people (a parent, a teacher, a peer) and ask for genuine, constructive criticism on a specific habit or past project. Accept it with humility (no defending yourself), document the physical/emotional stress you felt, and write a positivity plan to address the feedback.

Strategic Execution (7.5 Hrs)

The 30-Day Blueprint

Modules: Self Leadership, Setting Goals, Time Management, Strategic Planning, Delegation, Initiatives, Habits.

Blending Strategy

This block functions as a personal project incubator. Students pick a single "Initiative" they want to launch. Throughout the session, they apply Strategic Planning to build a roadmap, set SMART Goals, map out the Time Management required, and define the daily Habits needed to execute the plan, learning self-leadership by doing.

On-Field Assignment

Community Initiative Sprint: Take the initiative to organize a small, tangible project (e.g., organizing a neighborhood library shelf, a small bake sale, or a family cleanup day). Delegate at least two tasks to others, track your time strictly over 7 days, and report on the execution.

Total Allotted Time: 15 Hours

3 Specialist Teachers

Problem Solving (5 Hrs)

Design Thinking Hackathon

Modules: Observations, Structuring Problems, Mind Mapping, Analytical/Lateral Thinking, Innovation, Decision Making.

Blending Strategy

A rapid-fire hackathon. Students are given a complex, messy case study. They must use Critical Observation to find the real issue, structure the problem via Mind Mapping, apply Lateral Thinking to brainstorm off-the-wall innovations, and use Analytical Decision Making to select the final solution.

On-Field Assignment

Root-Cause Field Study: Identify a recurring inefficiency in your school or local community (e.g., canteen crowding, traffic drop-off). Map the root cause, develop an innovative lateral solution, and pitch the mapped decision to a school administrator.

Core Strategy (5 Hrs)

The Market Disruption Model

Modules: Business Mindset, Scaling, Fair Trade, Research, Trend-setting, Odyssey, Ecosystem.

Blending Strategy

Students step into the shoes of venture capitalists. They analyze an existing Business Ecosystem. They conduct simulated Market Research to find a gap, design a Trend-setting product, and develop a strategic plan for Scaling the business while maintaining Fair Trade principles.

On-Field Assignment

Bazaar Ethnography: Visit a local bustling market or bazaar. Observe one specific vendor. Diagram their complete business ecosystem (suppliers to buyers), perform a brief market analysis, and suggest one strategic scaling idea to the vendor.

Adv Enterprise (5 Hrs)

The Boardroom Simulation

Modules: Boldness, Compliance, Collaborative, Global, Financial Prudence, Hobbyist, Adaptive.

Blending Strategy

Operating as a board of directors, students take a "Hobbyist Enterprise" and map its trajectory to "Global Expansion". They must demonstrate Strategic Boldness in pitching partnerships (Collaborative Strategy), while rigorously proving Financial Prudence and strict legal Compliance in their expansion roadmap.

On-Field Assignment

The Micro-Enterprise Challenge: Start a 1-week Hobbyist Enterprise (e.g., selling handmade crafts or a digital service to friends/family). Maintain a prudent financial ledger, ensure ethical compliance, and write a reflection on adapting your approach based on sales.

Total Allotted Time: 15 Hours

3 Specialist Teachers

Ed-Theory (5.5 Hrs)

The Micro-Teaching Lab

Modules: Profession, Taxonomies, Planning, Assessment, Methods, Curriculum, Tech.

Blending Strategy

Students build a comprehensive curriculum slice. They define learning outcomes using Taxonomies, structure the Lesson Planning, choose diverse Teaching Methods, integrate Educational Technology, and design the final Assessment rubric—all in one fluid curriculum development cycle.

On-Field Assignment

Live Guest Teaching: Design a 15-minute lesson plan integrating one piece of Ed-Tech. Secure permission to teach it to a group of junior students (or peers), and assess their learning in real-time using Bloom's Taxonomy principles.

Classroom Practice (4 Hrs)

The Ecosystem Observation

Modules: Classroom Dynamics, Al-Muntalaq-ul-Islami, Teacher Ethics, Practicals 1 & 2.

Blending Strategy

A heavily practical workshop. The specialist sets up simulated disruptive classroom environments. Trainees must navigate these Dynamics while upholding Teacher Ethics and foundational principles (Al-Muntalaq). The session serves as guided Practicals for real-time classroom management.

On-Field Assignment

Teacher Shadowing: Shadow a veteran senior teacher for half a day. Document the unspoken classroom dynamics, identify specific instances where the teacher exercised ethical judgment, and write a reflective practical report.

Org Leadership (5.5 Hrs)

The Leadership Taskforce

Modules: Team Building, Inspiring, Collab, Mentor, Soft/Hard Leadership, Facilitation, Crisis.

Blending Strategy

The instructor acts as a "Crisis Facilitator". Students are grouped and thrown into a simulated organizational crisis. One student acts as the Mentor, utilizing Soft/Hard leadership to guide the team. They must practice advanced Facilitation Skills and cross-functional Collaboration to survive the scenario.

On-Field Assignment

Junior Facilitation: Act as a mentor and facilitate a 30-minute conflict-resolution, planning, or team-building session for a junior student council, a sports team, or scout troop. Document how you balanced soft and hard leadership styles.
